Pitcher XY Pad

Two continuous controls on a pad, one on an optional third slider. Pick any group from the list on the right and put it on X, Y or Z. The list is ranked by how strongly each group decides the sound's category in 1,719 real Surge presets. The coloured map behind the pad shows which category the sound leans towards at every point.

Groups

Ranked by how much the category guess drops when the group is scrambled (balanced accuracy, 8 categories). A grey bar means the effect is within the noise.

How this was made

    For the C++ version

    • Axis value 0–1 → knobs. Each knob in the group moves between its own 5th and 95th percentile in real presets, in the direction given by its sign. The pad never reaches settings presets don't use.
    • Groups are fixed lists of knobs. They're loaded from a table (name, knobs, signs, ranges), so the ranking and grouping can be updated without code changes.
    • Only continuous knobs. Switches and choices (oscillator type, filter type, play mode) stay as normal controls next to the pad.
    • The category map is optional. It needs a small classifier (here 60 decision trees, about 1 MB) evaluated on a grid when an axis changes.
    • The sound here is a rough browser synth, not Surge: it shows that moving the dot changes the sound, nothing more.
